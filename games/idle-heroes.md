## Core loop
- Collect heroes via summons, form a team, auto-battle through the campaign, collect idle rewards, upgrade heroes. [S]/[M]
- Campaign is the core source of gold, spirit and experience, plus equipment and consumables. [S]
- Idle rewards accrue offline up to about 8 hours; log in and collect at least every 8 hours. [S]
- This is a gacha collector; combat is automatic. [M]

## Resource types
- Gold, spirit, hero XP, equipment, hero shards. [S]
- Gems (premium), summon scrolls (Heroic Summon), energy for stages. [S]
- Shards come from campaign, Tower of Oblivion, events, daily quests. [S]

## Cost & production growth formulas (public or estimated; mark which)
- Not found. [E]
- Campaign is split into five difficulty levels: Normal, Nightmare, Hell, Death, Crazy; later ones pay more. [S]
- Hero star-up and level cost grow steeply; typical gacha design. [E]

## Prestige structure
- Hero merge/ascend and "reset" (rebirth) of heroes to return materials. [M] The Altar building disassembles unused heroes into spirit and promotion stones, higher-level ones also soul-stone shards that buy 5-star heroes [S https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/idle-heroes/idle-heroes-buildings-guide-en.html].
- Seasonal arenas and guild content reset by schedule. [M]
- No classic prestige loop. [M]

## Unlock pacing
- Campaign stages unlock in order; must clear previous to advance. [S]
- Modes (Tower, Arena, Temple, Guild) unlock via player level. [M]
- Events offer boosted summon rates. [S]

## Offline progress
- 8-hour cap on idle rewards [S bluestacks guides]. CONFLICT: an App Store review says non-spenders get 10 hours and VIP extends it [S https://apps.apple.com/us/app/idle-heroes-%EC%95%84%EC%9D%B4%EB%93%A4-%ED%9E%88%EC%96%B4%EB%A1%9C%EC%A6%88/id1153461915], so the cap may have changed by version; treat 8 h as unverified for the current build.

## Monetization effects
- Gacha summons, gem packs, monthly cards, event spend. [M] App Store lists a weekly subscription (about $0.99, regional) giving +100% auto-battle gold for a week plus an avatar [S https://apps.apple.com/DE/app/id1153461915].
- Advice to save gems for energy refills and events rather than random pulls. [S]

## What players love
- Large hero roster and automatic play. [M]
- Strong idle reward loop. [S]

## What players hate
- Gacha dependence and power creep. [M]
- Time pressure of the 8-hour cap. [S]

## Lessons for my game (Kamen Rider Decade idle, sequential Rider worlds, upgrade-focused, no fighting)
1. Linear campaign gate (must clear previous) with difficulty tiers is a ready-made world/era ladder without needing combat detail. [S]
2. A fixed offline cap with a "collect" button creates a daily rhythm; pick the cap deliberately (8 h here). [S]
3. Shard-based collection works without gacha: earn shards in-world to unlock a Rider. [S]
4. Five difficulty names per stage set give reuse of content at higher reward. [S]
5. Avoid gacha for a Decade game; use deterministic collection. [E]
6. Multiple modes unlocked by player level extends play without new worlds. [M]
