## Core loop
- Click/hold to blow leaves, sell leaves for coins, buy upgrades (blowers, tools, vacuums), unlock new leaf types (Basic -> ... Platinum -> Bismuth ...), add Areas, collect Gems, Pets, Cards, and Prestige for Prestige Coins / Gold Leaves.
- Multiple simultaneous mini-systems: Combos (Gold Leaf shop adds Combo), Cards (Card Parts), Pets, Boosters, Gem Upgrades. It is "kitchen sink incremental" with many unlockable subsystems.

## Resource types
- Coins, Leaves (by type), Gold Leaf (prestige?), Prestige Coins [S: "at first prestige, unlock Areas (20 coins), more Basic Leaves (2), more Gold Leaves (2), Prestige++ (5)"], Gems, Card Parts, Pet Food/Eggs, Nuclear Fuel (shop item), Combo.

## Cost & production growth formulas
- Gold Leaf shop (from wiki) [S]: Golden Marketing `500 * 3.5^n`; Max Combo `500 * 3^n`; Combo Multiplier `1,000 * 3^n` (+50% combo multiplier per level, ~624 shown max); Nuclear Fuel `100 * 1.5^n`.
- Basic Leaves shop item unlocks Gold Leaf for 10,000 basic leaves [S].
- Prestige first buys: Areas unlock 20 coins; a 10-coin area lets you progress to Bismuth instead of stopping at Platinum [S].
- Generic: costs follow `base * ratio^n` with ratio 1.5-3.5 for shop items depending on power [S pattern]. Standard upgrade price ratios ~1.1-1.2 for tools [E].
- Leaf values grow ~ x(5-10) per tier of leaf type [E].

## Prestige structure
- Prestige gives Prestige Coins spent on permanent shop items (Areas, More Leaves, Prestige++ that increases prestige gain) [S]. Gold Leaf shop is a separate "currency shop" using Gold Leaf. Later layers: Gems/Cards/Pets give meta-multipliers; "Super Prestige" style layers in later updates [M].
- Strategy: first prestige focus on unlocking areas to push beyond Platinum -> Bismuth [S].

## Unlock pacing
- Minute 0-10: blower & first upgrades; 30-60 min: new leaf types; first prestige in ~1-2 hr [E]. Days: cards, pets, gems layers. Weeks: late-game leaf types and mastery [E]. Steam game with regular content updates.

## Offline progress
- Yes, but modest: Gold leaf/ coins accumulate offline at reduced rate; upgraded via Gem upgrades/offline multipliers [M/E]. Game also supports auto-playing "no click" with auto-blowers.

## Monetization effects
- Paid on Steam (~$5) with no microtransactions; mobile versions free with ads and IAP [M]. Gems are earned in-game; gem upgrades are a core progression system.

## What players love
- Huge number of systems that each have clear incremental rewards; satisfying numbers; frequent updates; the "leaf type" progression gives a sense of world expansion; no pay-to-win on Steam.

## What players hate
- Overwhelming menus late game; some upgrades feel mandatory; grind for cards/gems; confusing prestige-vs-gold-leaf split for new players [S - wiki had to write a "No Gem Upgrade overall guide"].

## Lessons for my game (Kamen Rider Decade idle, sequential Rider worlds, upgrade-focused, no fighting)
1. Shop-formula families `base * ratio^n` with ratio keyed to power (1.5 cheap, 3-3.5 powerful) are easy to balance.
2. Use leaf-type tiers = Rider world tiers; unlocking a world in the prestige shop ("Areas 20 coins") is a satisfying first prestige goal.
3. A first-prestige shopping list should be surfaced by the game to avoid wiki dependency.
4. Combo mechanic offers moment-to-moment engagement without fighting.
5. Gate subsystems (cards, pets, gems) behind progress; do not show all at once.
6. Cap max-level for certain upgrades (e.g., Combo Multiplier cap) to create "done" moments.
