## Core loop
- Build a vault from rooms, assign dwellers by SPECIAL stats, keep power, food and water supplied, and survive incidents. [S]
- Production rooms depend on a SPECIAL stat; the dwellers' combined trait sets speed. [S]
- Idle-adjacent: rooms produce on timers, you tap to collect, and send dwellers to the wasteland. [M]

## Resource types
- Power, food, water (core). [S]
- Caps (currency), Nuka-Cola Quantum, lunchboxes, and pet/weapon/outfit items. [M]
- Dwellers (population) capped at 200. [S]

## Cost & production growth formulas (public or estimated; mark which)
- Rooms have three tiers (upgrade with caps); production is modified by room size, upgrade level, and dweller happiness. [S]
- Merge up to 3 identical rooms of the same level into a larger one; merged rooms hold up to 6 dwellers, are more efficient, but use more power and cost more to upgrade. [S]
- Room costs: fallout.wiki says rooms start at level 1 and upgrade with caps to level 3, 23 room types [S https://fallout.wiki/wiki/Fallout_Shelter_rooms]. Older Vault wiki says building more rooms of a type raises its base price while upgrade costs stay constant [S https://fallout-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Fallout_Shelter_rooms]; other sources say merged rooms cost more to upgrade (conflict). Exact numbers not found. [E]
- Population caps by living quarters. [S]

## Prestige structure
- No prestige in the base game. [M]
- A vault expansion is horizontal via room count and depth. [M]
- Disasters grow with room upgrade level; one guide warns upgrading raises incident strength "exponentially". [S]

## Unlock pacing
- 23 or 20 rooms depending on the source, unlocked by population growth. [S]
- Training rooms (7) unlock with dwellers; science/medbay as upgrades. [S]
- Quests and the wasteland add late goals. [M]

## Offline progress
- Sources conflict. Fallout wiki: resources drain at a constant rate and keep depleting for a short time after turning the game off, so keep a surplus [S https://fallout-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Fallout_Shelter_resources]. Forum (2017): production stops when closed, resource bars do not go down, timers (pregnancy, crafting, quests/exploration) still progress; dwellers exploring may die while away [S https://steamcommunity.com/app/588430/discussions/0/1354868867713037604]. No source found saying incidents happen while closed [S]. Mr. Handy collecting while away is a forum claim only [unconfirmed].

## Monetization effects
- Lunchboxes (random reward boxes) bought with real money; Nuka-Cola Quantum. [M]
- Production rooms can be rushed; the Destructoid review says a rush risks an incident (fire, radroach infestation) [S https://destructoid.com/?p=190054]. Rush success chance numbers not found. [M]

## What players love
- Charming art, the ant-farm view, and the risk-reward of rushing. [M]
- Low-friction base building. [M]

## What players hate
- Late-game stagnation, repetitive disasters, lunchbox monetization. [M]
- Incidents scale with upgrades, which punishes over-investment. [S]

## Lessons for my game (Kamen Rider Decade idle, sequential Rider worlds, upgrade-focused, no fighting)
1. Rooms-as-world-stand-ins: a vault grid where each room is a Rider world with a stat requirement is a no-combat structure. [E]
2. Merging identical rooms is a satisfying upgrade (3 -> 1 bigger) that avoids pure percentage upgrades. [S]
3. Tie unlocks to population (allies recruited) instead of time. [S]
4. Make a risk dial (rush) so players trade safety for speed. [M]
5. Do not let upgrades make incidents worse in a way that surprises players; a guide had to warn about it. [S]
6. Three-tier caps on a building keep the number space small and legible. [S]
