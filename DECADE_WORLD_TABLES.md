## Lull audit (worlds 4–6 — REVIEW_1 / anti_patterns lull)
- W4 Ryuki: one new card every ≈1–3 h of play (≈50 owned each) — beat density ≈ a pack per session.
- W5 Blade: fusion discoveries (≥10 recipes) gate mid-world; each found by combining, "knowledge" unlock (Increlution-style) so discovery ≈ every 2–4 h.
- W6 Kiva: day/night flip every 10 real minutes gives a built-in rhythm and a "night chest" per cycle (≤1.2× avg bonus, R38).
- Check with the campaign sim: worlds 4–6 at drift 1.2 were 4–6 days (v2) (longest dry streak 1–2 sessions); add ≥6 beats/session-day if lengthened further.

## Open design questions
Fusion rules complexity (Blade) must stay a 1-click UI; Rhythm (Hibiki) must never be required (R14); Kiva's day/night must not punish offline (offline uses average); Agito entirely unverified lore.
