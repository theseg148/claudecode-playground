# Decade source material (for theming)
Sources: https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Series/KamenRiderDecade ; https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Kamen_Rider_Decade
Show order of the nine Rider worlds: Kuuga, Kiva, Ryuki, Blade, Faiz, Agito, Den-O, Kabuto, Hibiki (user's game starts with Kabuto; order is free in an idle game).
Theme hooks usable as non-combat idle mechanics (author knowledge, not from fetched pages — verify before shipping):
- Kabuto: Clock Up (time speed), Cast Off armor, Zecter, Hyper Clock Up, ZECT, Worms; cooking/Sōji Tendō's home-life — "speed" and "cast-off" upgrades.
- Den-O: Imagin contracts, Time trains (DenLiner), time/past manipulation — "time travel" prestige.
- Ryuki: Advent cards, mirror world, contract monsters — card collection.
- Faiz: Photon blood/Orphnoch, phone transformation — resource-routing.
- Blade: Rouse cards/Undead, card fusion — collection/fusion upgrades.
- Kiva: Fangire/Kiva armor, castle/ Arms Monsters, Wakening — day/night cycle.
- Agito: Unknown/G3 units, Agito's Burning/Shining forms — form-upgrade tiers.
- Hibiki: Oni training, Ongeki instruments, Taiko — rhythm / training grinding.
- Kuuga: Arcle, Amadam, forms by colour — form-switch.
Decade core: Decade Driver, Kamen Ride / Attack Ride / Final Form Ride cards, Dimension Kick, Tsukasa's camera, Sub-characters (Natsumi, Yusuke, Kivala), Den-Liner "Hikari Studio" travel — framing: photograph each world to gain "Ride Cards".
