## Decade's own toolkit (the framing layer)
- Tsukasa Kadoya is an amnesiac photographer; his camera and photos are central; in the finale Daiki persuades Natsumi to destroy the camera and photos [S: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamen_Rider_Decade]. Photographer motif: HIGH confidence.
- Premise: travel nine worlds; early on he travels with Natsumi and her grandfather, later Yusuke Onodera (Kuuga world), Kivala (Kiva world), Daiki Kaito (Diend) [S: same page].
- Natsumi Hikari = Kanna Mori, also Kivala; grandfather = Eijiro Hikari (Renji Ishibashi); Yusuke Onodera = Ryota Murai; Daiki Kaito = Kimito Totani, "kaito" = phantom thief pun [S: https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Chinomanako (New Worlds characters list); https://villains.fandom.com/wiki/Daiki_Kaito]. Correct the earlier spelling: grandfather is Eijiro, not Eiji.
- Natsumi found Kiva-la items in rubble during the destruction of the worlds [S: https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Kamen_Rider_Decade_(Rider)]. Spellings Kiva-la / Kivala both appear.
- Decadriver + Rider Cards: Kamen Ride (assume form), Form Ride, Attack Ride, Final Attack Ride; Ride Booker is the main weapon (card book/sword) [S: https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Decadriver ; https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Ride_Booker].
- Final Form Ride cards transform another Rider into a combo form; "All Rider" does it to all nine [S: https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Final_Form_Rides].
- Dimension Kick exists, with NeoDecadriver upgrade mentioned [S: Decade (Rider) wiki above]. Toy belt came with 10 Kamen Ride cards [S: same].
- Diendriver is Daiki's device [S: https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Diendriver].
- Hikari Studio / Hikari Photo Studio: NOT confirmed in snippets [?]; [M] it is Natsumi's grandfather's photo studio and the home base; the studio's backdrop changes to each world (a Kiva Backdrop file exists on a wiki: https://kamenriderworld.fandom.com/wiki/File:Kiva_Backdrop.jpg -- suggestive only).
- Den-Liner as Decade's travel vehicle: [M]; unverified. (DenLiner itself is real, Den-O's train.)
- Kamen Ride Kuuga card power etc: [M].

## Per-world facts and idle ideas

### Kuuga
- Arcle belt powered by Amadam spirit stone, which also stabilizes the bearer [S: https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/File:KRKu-Arcle_(Rising_Forms).png ; https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Mighty_Form].
- Forms: Mighty, Dragon (Dragon Rod), Pegasus (Pegasus Bowgun), Titan (Titan Sword); Rising versions after electric shocks; Amazing Mighty with Mighty Anklets [S: same Mighty_Form page and search snippet].
- Yusuke Onodera is the Decade-world Kuuga and a travelling companion [S].
- Peaceful hook: [M] Kuuga's original cafe Pochi / Gokan... (the "Arcle cafe" search returned nothing [?]). Kuuga's Yusuke is a smiling traveller: "smile" currency [M].
- Idle upgrades: Mighty Anklets (passive x2), Rising Forms (tier ups), Amadam Polish (stability = offline cap), Pegasus Eye (see-far = auto-collect radius), Titan Plating.

### Kiva
- Kiva forms by Fuestles in Kivat's mouth; Emperor Form via Tatsulot; Fever finishers via Garulu Saber, Basshaa Magnum, Dogga Hammer [S: https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Kamen_Rider_Kiva_(Rider) ; toy-people.com/en/?p=72913 Emperor Form].
- Castle Doran is an ally device; Arms Monsters Jiro, Ramon, Riki; 13 Demon Races, merging "promotes peace" [S: same Kiva wiki results].
- Kivala (Natsumi) belongs here [S].
- Peaceful hooks: [M] violin workshop (Wataru makes violins), Cafe Marion Boar / Cafe Marion... "Marion" unverified; day/night (Kiva's 1986/2008 eras) [M].
- Idle upgrades: Violin Varnish, Fuestle Collection (set bonuses), Castle Doran Greenhouse, Kivat Duet, Emperor Tune.

### Ryuki
- 13 Card Decks (Advent Cards), Contract Monsters from the Mirror World, Contract Card forms a contract and unlocks more cards [S: https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Card_Decks_(Ryuki) ; Wikipedia Ryuki].

### Kabuto (special depth)
Decade arc:
- Tsukasa takes the role of a ZECTrooper and gains the ability to see Clock Up space [S: Decade search snippet #1; summary of Wikipedia/fandom; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamen_Rider_Decade].
- Decade world's Sohji (Tendo) is a Kabuto resident; he met the Phylloxera Worm (which mimicked him), his Clock Up system malfunctioned trapping him at warp speed, his sister Mayu held a grudge against Kabuto; Tsukasa's sacrifice restored him; Sohji later used Hyper Form to help against Dai-Shocker [S: https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Sohji].
- Episode titles seen: "Warning: Kabuto Running Amok" listed as ep. 16, with "The Grandma Way of Taste" cited alongside [S: Sohji page; Wikipedia episode list]. Exact ep numbers for World of Kabuto not confirmed; [M] roughly eps 15-16 -- do not state in game.
- Hyper Kabuto vs Dai-Shocker is a late-series cameo [S].
Original Kabuto lore:
- Sōji Tendō trained seven years waiting for the Kabuto Zecter; wants to destroy all Worms; works with ZECT on his own terms [S: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamen_Rider_Kabuto ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Kamen_Rider_Kabuto_characters].
- Arata Kagami = Gatack (son of ZECT leader Riku Kagami) [S].
- Zecters (Masked Rider System): eight known; TheBee, Drake, Sasword named; Sasword is based on an arachnid [sic, snippet claim; original is scorpion-themed, so [M] scorpion]; Hercus is based on a Hercules beetle and uses Kabutick Zecter (Henshin Brace) [S: https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Masked_Rider_System ; https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Gatack_Zecter]. Kabuto, Gatack, Drake = [M] (Kabuto beetle, stag beetle? Gatack is a stag beetle, Drake is dragonfly) -- Gatack Zecter page exists [S].
- Tenshi: [?] not found. "Misaki Kaoru": [?] not found; Kabuto characters are not confirmed to include it; remove. Hiyori Kusakabe and Souichi Kusakabe: Souichi page exists [S: https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Souichi_Kusakabe]; Hiyori [M].
- ZECT formed to counter Worms from a meteorite; Worms are alien lifeform [S].
- Clock Up: accelerates particles around the user so they move freely within the stream of time [S: https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Hyper_Form].
- Cast Off: shout "Cast Off!" and press Zecter switch; Zecter purges armor from Masked Form to Rider Form; armor flies off at up to 2 km/s; Put On reattaches, even partially (ep. 24) [S: zh.moegirl.org.cn Cast_Off pages; Hyper_Form page].
- Hyper Form: via Hyper Zecter; Hyper Clock Up lets one go faster than Clock Up or alter time/other dimensions; Hyper Cast Off line "Change Hyper Beetle" (fan wiki); used by Kabuto, and Gatack in Hyper Battle DVD; Kamen Rider Caucasus uses Hyper Zecter in God Speed Love movie [S: https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Hyper_Form].
- Peaceful hooks: [S] Grandma's taste (episode title "The Grandma Way of Taste"); [M] Sōji's cooking (Tendō's grandmother-trained gourmet, "Sōji is a cook" -- the original uses the hotel/restaurant Bistro La Salle), his sister Mayu (Decade) [S for Mayu]; keep cooking and kitchen as the main idle motif.
- Idle upgrades (no fighting): Clock Up Prep Station (x2 speed for timed actions, cooldown), Hyper Clock Up (x10 bursts / time skip), Cast Off Mise-en-place (instant batch unlock; "Cast Off" = shed an old tier for a new one, then Put On = re-equip), Zecter Roster (collect 8 insects as workers: Kabuto, Gatack, Drake, Sasword, Hercus, TheBee, Tenshi?; each a worker type), Bistro Menu (Grandma's Recipes), ZECT Logistics (Worm-free delivery), Phylloxera Mirror (prestige reset that copies your build).
