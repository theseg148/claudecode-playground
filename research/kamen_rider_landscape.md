# Kamen Rider landscape for a Decade-inspired idle/collection game

Date of research: 2026-10-18. Method: WebSearch (standard) only; no page was fetched in full, so claims rest on search-result summaries.
Legend: [S]+URL = supported by a search result (URL is the result's source); [M] = my own inference or background knowledge, not verified this session; UNVERIFIED = searched and not found.
This is research, not legal advice. No policy text is quoted below; where a policy is described it is paraphrased from a search summary.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
## 0. Bottom line
1. No Toei / Ishimori Productions / Bandai fan-game policy was found in any search. Treat "no published policy" as "no permission", not as "allowed". [S] absence of results (several queries, EN and JP); inference [M].
2. No takedown history for a Kamen Rider fan game was found. That is absence of evidence only; Nintendo's mass takedowns on Game Jolt show the type of action rights holders take against fan games. [S] https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2021/01/nintendo_issues_mass_dmca_takedown_379_fan-made_games_forcibly_removed
3. The Decade card motif (Rider Cards, Decadriver, Ride Booker) is verifiable and well documented, but it is also the most recognisable, merchandised part of the IP. Use the structure (card types as verbs), not the names or designs.
4. The only official Kamen Rider user-content guideline located is for Bandai's Ride Kamens app, and it covers that app only. [S] https://dengekionline.com/article/202405/6556 (via JP search summary). It does not extend to third-party games.
5. Safest route: an original-IP game whose mechanics echo "dimension-hopping collector with cards" without Kamen Rider names, silhouettes, logos, music, or screenshots.

### 1.3 Observations useful for design
- Existing licensed games are overwhelmingly combat-centred (fighters, beat-em-ups, auto-battlers) with gacha for roster. No licensed idle/incremental Kamen Rider game surfaced. [S] absence across searches; the genre looks open [M].
- The card-as-mechanic lineage is strongest in toys and arcade (Ganbaride + Decadriver) rather than mobile. The toy review notes cards came packaged with toys or from vending machines, and that the DX Decadriver plays generic sounds for unrecognised Ganbaride cards. [S] https://www.collectiondx.com/toy_review/2009/henshin_belt_dx_decadriver
- Collection-through-progress (cards earned as you play) existed in Climax Heroes, which supports a non-gacha, earn-by-playing collection loop as familiar to the fandom. [S] playpile URL above, with the caveat that it concerns a predecessor.
- Ride Kamens's shutdown after about 1.5 years (announced for 2025-10-31; launch date not verified here) is a data point that licensed gacha-adjacent mobile titles can be short-lived. Cause not stated; do not infer. [S] egw.news URL above.
- Fan-made clickers/idle: searched itch.io and general web; none found. UNVERIFIED (search engine coverage of itch.io is weak). A Kamen Rider fan zine exists on itch.io, with a disclaimer that characters belong to Toei and the work is non-profit and unofficial. [S] https://rider-system-zine.itch.io/rider-system-a-kamen-rider-zine
- Fan-made rider concepts (e.g. "Kamen Rider Zein") exist as community creations, not officially recognised. [S] https://blog.blog.wordpress.arktimes.com/?p=27136 (low-quality blog; weak source).
- Reception data (reviews, scores, ratings) for the mobile titles was not found. UNVERIFIED. Recommend checking App Store / Google Play ratings and Japanese review sites manually.

### 1.4 Monetisation context (general, not Kamen Rider-specific)

## 2. IP / legal landscape

### 2.1 What was and was not found
- Toei fan-work / fan-game guideline: NOT FOUND in EN or JP searches. [S] search summaries said no Toei guideline located; https://www.toy-people.com/jp/?p=104845 appeared but was not confirmed to be one.
- Ishimori Productions fan-work policy: NOT FOUND (JP search summary explicitly says no information confirmed).
- Bandai policy: only the Ride Kamens UGC guideline (May 2024) was found. Described as from Bandai's card division, with Ishimori Pro and Toei cooperation, covering novels, illustrations, manga, video, cosplay, and game streaming, with prohibitions on content contrary to public order and morals and on religious/political content. [S] https://dengekionline.com/article/202405/6556 and https://news.denfaminicogamer.jp/news/2405312k (paraphrased from search summary; I did not read the primary text). Scope: that app's content only [M].
- Takedown history for Kamen Rider fan games: NOT FOUND. Two searches returned only Nintendo/Capcom/Vivendi/Square Enix cases. [S] https://gamesbeat.com/nintendo-allegedly-files-dmca-takedown-notice-over-more-than-550-fans-games/ ; https://dashfight.com/news/capcom-slaps-fan-game-with-cease-desist-for-using-character-likeness
- Do not cite "Toei is lenient" or "Toei is strict": neither is supported by evidence gathered. [M]

### 2.2 Comparable policies from other Japanese publishers (for calibration only)
- Nitroplus: allows fan creations including doujinshi if not "excessively commercial"; earlier guidelines gave numeric limits (cumulative sales of 200 units or under, expected revenue under 100,000 yen), later moved toward fan judgment. [S] https://automaton-media.com/en/news/send-us-your-doujinshi-nitroplus-affirms-positive-stance-on-fan-made-content-asks-people-to-show-them-what-theyre-cooking/
- Capcom: generally prohibits commercial use, with an exception for small-scale physical doujin books/magazines. [S] https://automaton-media.com/en/?p=53641
- KEMCO: fan creations for personal or limited-circle use; fan merchandise prohibited. [S] https://www.kemco.jp/guideline/fan_movie_decodeco_en.html
- Touhou (ZUN): revised guidelines banned sales of 2D animations; standard doujin distribution largely unaffected. [S] https://www.gamebusiness.jp/article/2026/09/05/27910.html (JP summary; check original)
- Takeaway: where policies exist, they emphasise non-commercial, small-scale, no official logos/assets. Their existence for those companies says nothing about Toei. [M]

### 2.3 Japanese doujin conventions (general)
- Rights holders in Japan rarely sue fan creators; the arrangement is tolerant but legally ambiguous. [S] https://copyrightevidence.org/wiki/index.php/Arai_and_Kinukawa_(2014) and https://ejournals.eu/en/journal/gsaw/article/wolnosc-tworcza-a-prawo-autorskie-doujinshi-we-wspolczesnej-japonii (scholarly summaries).
- Forum-level folk rules: no official logo, no mass production, no large profit. This is anecdotal. [S] https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/bbs/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?p=5179619 (forum; low reliability).
- Enforcement depends on the rights holder and scale. [S] same scholarly sources; [M] for the "scale" generalisation.
- A tolerated doujin book at a convention differs from a free-to-play mobile game with ads or IAP distributed globally. [M] Monetised distribution via app stores is the risky tier.
- Nintendo's Game Jolt takedowns reportedly cited images of characters and ad revenue on game pages as a likely trigger. [S] nintendolife URL in section 0 (the "ad revenue" motive is the outlet's interpretation).

### 2.4 Risk matrix (my assessment [M], not policy)

| Element | Risk | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Original art, original names, original story | Low | The only fully safe tier |
| Generic descriptive reference in a README ("inspired by tokusatsu rider shows") | Low-Medium | Nominative/descriptive; avoid implying endorsement; keep it out of the app name and store keywords |
| Using "Kamen Rider" or "Decade" in the title / store metadata | High | Trademark and keyword confusion |
| Official logos, belt designs, rider silhouettes, helmet shapes | High | Core merchandising assets |
| Ripped sprites, screenshots, music, SFX, voice clips | Very high | Direct copying |
| Card layouts imitating Ganbaride/Rider Cards (magenta/barcode style, rider faces) | High | Recognisable trade dress [M] |
| Mechanic ideas (collect cards, spend to transform, charge a finisher) | Low | Game mechanics are generally not protected as such in many jurisdictions [M, not verified] |
| Monetisation (IAP/ads/Patreon) on fan work | High | Every comparator policy above restricts commercial use |
| Free, non-commercial, clearly unofficial, open source | Medium | Still unauthorised; no safe harbour found |
| Seeking permission from Toei/Ishimori | n/a | The only way to get certainty; contact route not verified |

### 2.5 Disclaimers practice
- The itch.io Rider zine uses a "characters belong to Toei; non-profit; unofficial" disclaimer. [S] rider-system-zine URL above. A disclaimer is a courtesy, not a legal shield. [M]

## 3. Decade-specific motifs (verified items only)

Source reliability note: nearly all detail below comes from the fan wiki and toy-review sites via search summaries; the episode guide on Wikipedia was returned for plot items. Treat as community-sourced.

### 3.1 Verified facts
- Tsukasa Kadoya uses the DecaDriver belt and Rider Cards to become Kamen Rider Decade. Card types named: Kamen Ride, Form Ride, Attack Ride, Final Attack Ride. [S] https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Kamen_Rider_Decade_(Rider)
- Final Form Ride: Decade transforms another rider into a new form, for a combo attack; Decade reaches into the rider's back, while Diend shoots them in the back; affected riders can alternate between forms while the card is in effect. [S] https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Final_Form_Rides
- Final Attack Ride: holographic constructs act as portals through which the attack travels. [S] https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Decadriver (summary wording)
- Kamen Ride cards transform into Decade and the previous nine Heisei riders. [S] playpile / fandom results above.
- Ride Booker: Decade's weapon and card storage; used with Rider Cards to fight using other riders' armaments. [S] https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Ride_Booker
- Dimension Kick: usable via the Ride Booker in Book Mode as a flying side kick; Attack Ride Illusion creates three clones that all perform it. [S] fandom results (Dimension Kick, Ride Booker pages); fan wiki, lower authority.
- Plot: Natsumi Hikari works at the Hikari photo studio with her grandfather Eijiro; Tsukasa's photos come out bad and he says he does not belong in this world; reality rifts open and monsters from earlier riders' worlds appear. [S] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Kamen_Rider_Decade_episodes and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamen_Rider_Decade
- "Destroyer of Worlds": Narutaki and others believe Tsukasa is the destroyer; the stakes are nine universes merging into one. [S] same Wikipedia/fandom pages.
- Companions: Yusuke Onodera (World of Kuuga), Kivala (Natsumi in The Last Story), Daiki Kaito/Diend (cards and a gun-based version of Final Form Ride). [S] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamen_Rider_Decade
- Eijiro Hikari is revealed as a guise of Dr. Shinigami of Dai-Shocker. [S] https://kamenrider.fandom.com/wiki/Eijiro_Hikari (spoiler).

### 3.3 Translating motifs to non-combat idle mechanics
Mapping is [M] design suggestion; each row lists the verified motif it abstracts.

| Source motif | Abstracted verb | Idle mechanic |
|---|---|---|
| Kamen Ride (become another rider) | Assume an identity | "Persona/role cards": equip a card to switch the producing role of your main generator; each role has a different resource curve |
| Attack Ride (use a rider's ability) | Trigger an ability | Active "burst" cards: spend a charge for a timed production multiplier or an instant payout |
| Form Ride (change form) | Change form | Mode switch: toggle between "steady" (offline-friendly) and "surge" (active-play) forms |
| Final Form Ride (transform a partner) | Empower an ally | Partner-boost: consume a card to evolve an automation helper into a stronger form for a window of time |
| Final Attack Ride (finisher via portals) | Chain-finisher | Prestige/ascension: line up N cards in sequence, trigger a "grand finale" that resets progress for a permanent multiplier |
| Ride Booker (card storage) | Binder | Collection album with set bonuses and a visible completion % |
| Rider cards from many worlds | Collect from worlds | World tiers: each "world" is a themed zone with its own set; unlocking the next resets the pace |
| Dimension Kick (portal-guided kick) | Line-up strike | Visual flourish for the prestige event; no combat required |
| Tsukasa photography / Hikari studio | Capture moments | A "photo mode" idle: take snapshots of each world to unlock album pages (original-art snapshots, not real stills) |
| Rifts opening between worlds | Cross-world events | Timed "rift" events that add temporary generators from other worlds |
| "Destroyer of worlds" framing | Ambivalent hero | Optional narrative flavour: ascension "merges worlds" for a bonus; keep wording original |

Design notes [M]:
- Non-combat idle loops: passive production -> upgrade -> unlock new world -> prestige. Cards act as modifiers, which maps cleanly onto Kamen Ride/Attack Ride semantics.
- Collection loop: cards earned by playing (as in Climax Heroes [S] playpile) rather than purchased, to sidestep gacha controversy and keep it non-commercial.
- Avoid a fighting layer to keep distance from the franchise's core (all found licensed games are fighters).

## 4. Naming and rebranding guide ("inspired-by" without the IP)

Principle [M]: keep the mechanical idea, replace every proper noun, silhouette, colour code, and sound. Do not use names that are a phonetic or pun variant of protected terms.

### 4.1 Term mapping (suggested originals)

| Franchise term | Avoid | Suggested original |
|---|---|---|
| Kamen Rider | any use, incl. "Masked Rider", "Kamen" | "Wayfarer", "Vanguard" (generic hero) |
| Decade | "Decade", "10-year" puns | "Cartographer", "Aperture" |
| DecaDriver | "Driver", "Decadriver", "Deca-" | "Deck Dial", "Slot Frame" |
| Ride Booker | "Booker", "Ride Book" | "Binder", "Folio" |
| Rider Card | "Rider Card", "Ride Card" | "World Card", "Portal Card" |
| Kamen Ride | "Kamen Ride", "X Ride" naming scheme | "Assume", "Persona" |
| Attack Ride | "Attack Ride" | "Skill Card", "Burst" |
| Form Ride | "Form Ride" | "Stance", "Mode" |
| Final Form Ride | "Final Form Ride" | "Fusion", "Overdrive" |
| Final Attack Ride | "Final Attack" | "Grand Finale", "Ascension" |
| Dimension Kick | "Dimension Kick", "Rider Kick" | "Rift Strike", "Prism Drop" |
| Destroyer of Worlds | the phrase | "World Weaver" or omit |
| Hikari Studio / Tsukasa | character names | original protagonist and studio |
| Ganbaride | anything | omit |
| Heisei Riders, Shocker, Dai-Shocker, Den-O | all names | omit |

The pattern "X Ride" across all card names is a distinctive naming scheme in the show [S] fandom results; do not adopt a "<word> Ride" series. [M]

### 4.2 Visual and audio
- Do not use a belt-with-buckle motif as logo; use abstract card frames with your own colours. Avoid magenta-and-barcode treatment closely associated with the franchise's cards [M, from toy imagery not verified here].
- Do not use rider helmet silhouettes (large compound eyes plus antennae). Use abstract world sigils instead.
- No transformation jingles, voice clips ("Kamen Ride!"), or music. Create original SFX.
- Original palette; avoid direct reproduction of any suit colourway.

### 4.3 Metadata and communication
- App title, subtitle, package ID, and store keywords: no franchise terms.
- README "inspired by" line: a single, factual line such as "Inspired by card-collecting tokusatsu hero shows" is lower risk than naming the show; if you name it, do so only in prose, never in title/tags, with a "not affiliated with or endorsed by Toei, Ishimori Productions, or Bandai" notice. [M] A notice does not create a license.
- Do not promote on franchise hashtags or use fan-art tags.
- No monetisation tied to franchise-like content; if monetised, use only the original-IP tier. [M]
- Keep source code and assets in a repo with an explicit original-asset licence; record provenance for every asset.

### 4.4 Process checklist
1. Write an original world bible (names, sigils, palette) before any art.
2. Name-check every term against Toei/Bandai product lines and a trademark search (J-PlatPat, USPTO TESS) [M; not performed].
3. Review each mechanic as "verb + resource", never "franchise term + effect".
4. Ask a lawyer familiar with Japanese IP if distribution is commercial or widespread.
5. If a tribute is wanted, consider asking Toei/Ishimori for written permission; contact channel not verified.
